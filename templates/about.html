Our mission

Threat intelligence
made understandable.

ThreatLense is a cybersecurity threat intelligence dashboard designed to help people recognize emerging risks, understand why they matter, and decide what to do next.

The purpose

From scattered data to useful context

Cybersecurity teams receive information from many sources. Raw alerts alone can be noisy and difficult to prioritize. ThreatLense organizes that data into clear threat briefs with severity, confidence, category, and response guidance.

This IT401 project demonstrates how a focused web application can turn complex security information into an approachable, decision-ready view.
